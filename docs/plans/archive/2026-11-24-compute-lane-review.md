Ideas kept:
- Resume mechanics. A 1d unit is one 25-name chunk over the whole span (515 units for 12,857 names); a batch key hashes writer, tf, range, symbols, code key, APR snapshot and input digest, so an edit to any hashed module renames every unit while a throughput key (workers, BLAS threads, block rows) does not. A killed unit leaves a `started` row and no rows; resume with the same run id takes it over and skips completed units. A kill loses at most the group loading and the group queued behind it (about 50 names, 2 to 3 minutes of compute, 1 to 2 minutes of replanning).
- Kill procedure. `kill -TERM` the whole `setsid` process group, confirm no forkserver process remains, terminate any active COPY backend, keep the six timers quiesced (a restated bar changes input digests and completed units stop matching), relaunch the same `--run-id` from the run worktree. Pauses under the 1,200 s limit are safe.
- The stored 53 h `stage_1d` extrapolation is inflated: the failed pilot's chunk time of 370 s includes a one-off 317 s `compression_bound`, which the extrapolation counted 515 times; the measured compute was 52 s for 25 names. The drafter's estimate at 4 workers is 6 to 10 hours, unmeasured, so the pilot's `corpus_run.measured` is the budget.
- Stale rows are the certain failure: the rebuild is append-only (no `replace_where` on load), so rows left by an earlier run's units collide on `feature_vectors_pkey (symbol, tf, bar_ts)`; supersede the provenance rows (migration 416 transition) and delete the unit's rows first. Checked 2026-11-25: `feature_vectors` 1d holds 3,061,660 rows over 1,509 names and run f9f69166 has 65 completed units at the old key.
- Levers that stay proposals (all move the key or stored values, so each needs its own pin and a ledger line): SMC order-block scan (up to about 15 percent of compute, must be bit-exact against the golden production-path test), shared pivot input (about 7 percent), HMM warm starts (up to about 25 percent, breaks the expanding-prefix design choice), HMM retry continuation (unmeasured), price percentile (2.5 percent). Levers that do not move the key: workers 4 to 8 (3 to 5 hours, thermal gate), a stage launcher, the 65-name pilot (about 1 hour, units are kept), keeping `blas_threads_per_worker` at 1 and chunk size at 25.
- Invariant gaps noted: dividend rows and the restated reference ETF bars (SPY, TLT, SHY, TIP, HYG, LQD) change a unit's inputs without renaming it, so only quiesced timers protect a kill and resume; `_TRADING_EXCHANGE` and the reference ETF list are US-equity assumptions.
