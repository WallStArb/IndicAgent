# Pre-registration: family 2, overnight versus intraday return decomposition

Original: 2026-09-25 (registered after two adversarial review rounds; never run).
Archived 2026-10-31: voided-era prereg, results voided by the owner's 2026-10-30 data reset; superseded by the 2026-10-27 daily residual momentum reversal prereg.

Hypothesis (Lou, Polk and Skouras 2019; Berkman et al. 2012): overnight (close-to-open) and
intraday (open-to-close) residual-return legs carry different, partly opposite premia from
different clienteles; attention-driven buying lifts the open and the stock gives some of it
back during the day. Six members on one 09:45 signal row targeting the rest of the session,
187 US-session names; gap fade members are disclosed seen-data re-specifications.

Never produced a real-data number; the evidence run was queued, not run. Re-testable as
written once the intraday corpus is clean. The overnight-target half (enter close, exit next
open) is still untested and needs a target the runner does not yet express.
