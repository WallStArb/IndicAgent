### Causes

By pattern over the 798 tickers: never placed 467 tickers (23,215 months, 99 live at L); partly placed with a CIK change 88 (5,099 months, 28 unplaced at L); partly placed with one CIK 243 (7,181 months, 1 unplaced at L).

- Existing instruments the earlier FIGI and events map could not place. The map's own gap list (`gap_list.csv`, scope `instrument`) names the reason: of the 128 unplaced at L, 121 are on it: `massive_no_figi` 94 (ACN, AON, SW), `event_chain_head_mismatch` 17 (A: the Massive events chain for A has head AWD), `asset_class_outside_vendor` 6, `no_span_survived` 3, `events_malformed` 1. The pit install is additive, so an existing instrument with no identity stayed without a span although a pit run of its own ticker and CIK exists.
- Records with no CIK and no FIGI. Of 363 never-placed tickers not on the gap list, 249 carry no CIK in any month and 239 no FIGI; they are unplaced by the key rule (key kind needs a FIGI or a CIK); 107 appear in one month only; only 38 are instruments and 7 are live at L. This is the already disclosed residual (unplaced share 0.0442, counted in ticker-years, not by name).
- CIK change. The premise that a CIK change across lists is the cause is mostly false. Across the active lists there are 1,281 CIK changes (1,073 tickers): 852 follow a gap, 323 are contiguous with a similar name, 102 contiguous with a dissimilar name, 4 change exchange. In 1,227 of them (96 percent) both sides are placed (the map keeps each CIK run as its own security, `X~cik`). Only 54 changes leave a side unplaced (41 after the change, 11 before, 2 both); 15 of the 41 are contiguous with a similar name, 12 of those live (ACN 2009-09, AU, BATRA, BG, BK, BZH, DUK, ETN, FWONK, MDT, MSG, NE).
- SW is a different case: WRK (CIK 1732845) to SW (CIK 2005951) is a ticker change with a new CIK, and SW has no span although the list holds SW from 2024-07-31. It is on the gap list as `massive_no_figi`, so it is the first cause, and the A3 sweep's identity line for SW 2016-05-16 clears when SW is placed from 2024-07-08.

## 2. Evaluation of a continuity rule

A rule that merges across a CIK change (same ticker, contiguous month-ends, same exchange, name token overlap of at least 0.5 after legal suffixes and class words are dropped) acts on 323 changes. On the 624 reused-ticker population in the same data it is correct on the three CIK-change cases it can see: FAF 2010 (spin-off child reuses the ticker; contiguous, same exchange, name overlap 0.25, so not merged), SN 2019 to 2023 (gap, not merged), HLT 2007 to 2013 (gap, not merged). The other 624 cases (THRM, SSB) are inactive-list cases outside this test. With 0 of 3 wrong the false-positive rate is not estimated; the upper 95 percent bound is 63 percent. A name-overlap threshold also fails on legal renames (ASH to Ashland Global, ANGI, APA, all dissimilar). Conclusion: a merge-across-CIK rule is not needed for the bulk and its false-positive rate cannot be shown, so it is not proposed as an automatic rule; the 15 contiguous-similar cases with an unplaced side are a hand-reviewed list.

## 3. Proposed amendment (for the owner)

Cause, found in `src/intelligence/bars/ticker_entity_map_pit.py` (`plan_pit_map`, the `spanless` branch): an instrument with no live span "still owns its ticker string", so every pit run of that ticker with the instrument's key is dropped as `already_live` ("the key's active run is the instrument ACN", 1,901 such rows in `span_accounting.csv`). The rule was written to avoid a second suffixed instrument for the same company; it assumed the instrument would get its spans from a later amendment, and none did. Verified on ACN, A, AON, SW, QGEN, ST and STE; all 112 P1 names carry an unretired composite FIGI, share-class FIGI and ISIN, so a span can attach under the existing identifier.

Rule P1: for an equity instrument that has no live span and is on the gap list for `massive_no_figi`, `event_chain_head_mismatch` or `events_malformed`, place every pit run of its ticker that has the instrument's key, as `ticker_event` spans under the instrument's existing composite FIGI identifier, each from the run's first month-end (the archive start when the run is in the first snapshot) to its end (open at L), minus any live span. Not only the latest run: FISV has a 237-month run before its ticker change and would otherwise keep 80 unplaced months (NESR, GLIBA, MPWR and AEIS are similar). Identity condition: when the run carries a composite FIGI it must equal the instrument's composite FIGI; when it carries none (89 of the 94 `massive_no_figi` names, whose runs are keyed by CIK and ticker), the link is the ticker string, the CIK and a name match, and the instrument's name must overlap the run's latest name (all 112 do, 0.5 or better). Two names fail the FIGI condition and are not P1 cases: CRH (run key BBG000BBLR09, the 2006 to 2023 ADR, bridged by CIK into the ordinary shares; the instrument is BBG000D3JXM1) and FTAI (run key BBG005T0KPY1, the Delaware LLC; the instrument is the post-2022 Cayman Ltd, BBG019M62R45). They go on the hand list, so P1's scope is 110 of the 128. Deterministic from the lists and the live identifiers, no returns or prices. Evidence per span names the rule, the run's key and CIK, and the instrument.

A ticker rename under one composite FIGI already merges into one security in this planner (FISV to FI, NEXN to TRMR, PSKY to SKYD), while a rename under a CIK-only key does not (NU to ES under CIK 72741): an asymmetry of the existing key rule, not changed here. P1 places the same-ticker runs only; the renamed-ticker runs of these instruments (FI 29 months, TRMR 31, SKYD to L) are listed in the hand list.

## 4. Mechanism, re-run cost and effects

- Not the `--amend` path. `--amend` takes a returns-based witness for span gaps; P1 has none. The existing vehicle is the pit install: `--pit-anchor` builds a scratch map version and `--pit-install` writes it (rehearsal by default, a proven rollback, `--rollback INSTALL_ID`). The code does not handle spans on an existing instrument's identifier yet: `install_pit_plan` looks identifiers up among the ones it just inserted (a KeyError for an existing instrument), and `plan_rollback`, `retire_install_identifiers` and the identifier count in `_prove_rollback` assume new symbols. Code after adoption, test first: the planner change (P1 spans, `spanless` restricted to equities), the install and rollback changes, tests, independent review. More than the 150 lines first estimated; not started, because the owner may decline.
- A raw load and a canonical rebuild come BEFORE the membership gain exists. These instruments' canonical 1d bars are Alpaca only (2016-01-04 on, IBKR to 2003-09-09); there is no Massive row under the instrument symbol, because the all-ticker load wrote raw only for placed spans. After the install, a Massive 1d load for the new spans (the 110 names and the ten futures-collision equities, through `ops_massive_load --all-tickers --gap-list <new map>` as `run_609.sh` s4 does), then canonical selection, the split audit and the derivation, then the 647 audit. The membership writer refuses when canonical is not current with splits. Consequence for rollback: `--rollback` refuses once load rows cite the new spans (`--accept-loaded-rows`), so after the raw load the install is not cleanly reversible; that is the point of no return and is named in the runbook.
- Re-run order and cost: map version build (minutes, to be measured on the rehearsal), install rehearsal then apply (the `ticker_event` lock, about 2 to 10 minutes; about 1,000 span rows), the Massive 1d load for the new spans, selection and derivation (hours not measured; the first all-ticker 1d load wrote about 50M raw rows for 33,850 names), the 647 audit (21 s), migration 605 and the writer (156 s rehearsal and 232 s apply on 2026-11-19), then s6 and s7 re-read. The s3 stage is not re-run; the new install is a second, additive one.
- Membership: `universe_membership` and its ledger are insert only (migration 597: a correction is a dated amendment and a new build), so the old rows are copied to `universe_membership_pre605` and `universe_membership_build_pre605` (with the insert-only triggers) and the live tables are truncated by migration 605, which refuses unless the live build is the measured one (858,454 rows, 278 month-ends) and no `research_run` exists. It fails loudly once a 2026-11-30 month is appended, so the reset must precede that. Counts move by up to about 110 names in the later years (728 name-years at the tier entry over the whole history).
- Reruns the new members need: the closed-list digest pin (below), `integrity_monitor` `proven_start_1d` facts (the 647 audit's `--apply` family; see `docs/plans/2026-11-20-member-proven-start.md`), and the strict sweep scope (the sweep reads `universe_membership`, so the new members are unswept until it reruns).
- The closed-list digest pin (`CLOSED_LIST_CANDIDATES_SHA256`, 13,103 lines, `e13546aa78be46026b204ea5de46cbd09a181c177f6a14cc206cbbeaf3b1b29c`) changes: the candidate set at L gains about 110 symbols. It is repinned in the same dated amendment from the post-install data, before any closed-list month is built; the pin tests and the report line follow. The pin stays pre-registered, not APR.
- Gates: independent review of the map version (every name above $100M a day and a sample of the rest), the member-history audit and the unplaced reach scripts rerun (this document's numbers are the before), `repro_frozen` untouched because no file under `src/intelligence/research/` or `statistics/` moves. Runbook: `docs/operations/stage2-membership-rebuild-runbook.md`.
