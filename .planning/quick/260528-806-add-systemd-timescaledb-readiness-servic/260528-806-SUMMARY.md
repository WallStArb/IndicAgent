# Phase 260528-806 Plan 01: Add systemd TimescaleDB Readiness Service Summary

**One-liner:** TimescaleDB pg_isready oneshot + indicagent-infrastructure.target aggregate gate wired to all 40 app services, eliminating reboot-time connection-refused noise.

## What Was Built

Two new systemd unit files introduce a DB readiness gate that mirrors the existing Redpanda pattern:

- `indicagent-timescaledb-ready.service` - oneshot that polls `pg_isready` every 5s for up to 300s (60 attempts), then succeeds with "TimescaleDB ready".
- `indicagent-infrastructure.target` - aggregate target that `Requires=` both the timescaledb-ready and redpanda-ready oneshots. All app services depend on this single target.

All 40 app service files received `Requires=indicagent-infrastructure.target` and `After=...indicagent-infrastructure.target`. Excluded: `indicagent-redpanda-ready.service`, `indicagent-timescaledb-ready.service`, `indicagent-redpanda-watchdog.service`.
