2. **SNR == selection_rate presented as a percentage.** Do not invent a separate SNR computation — D-05 defines SNR as selection_rate.

3. **setup_performance and swarm_agent_weights are empty/stale by design** after the replay (they repopulate on nightly ml-training 11pm and weekly ml-orchestrator Monday). The report must NOTE this, not treat it as a failure.

4. **Use the frozenset for cluster membership.** The 22 `_SHADOW_VALIDATION_SETUPS` are NEEDS_REFACTOR (now refactored, in shadow). The 8 non-shadow setups are the GOOD/MODERATE control group. Do not hardcode cluster lists by memory — derive shadow membership from `is_shadow` in the query and the frozenset.
</critical_corrections>
