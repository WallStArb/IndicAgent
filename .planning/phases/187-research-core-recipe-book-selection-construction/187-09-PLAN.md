<objective>
Three combining rules from design 4.1 and 4.2, all new optional combiner fields whose unset value reproduces today exactly (design 14.6 rule 1: behavior changes are separate commits from refactors, and each is a commit of its own here).

1. Missing members (R-187-16). `EqualWeight.combine` is complete-case across members, so uneven coverage silently shrinks the cross-section. With `min_members` set, each member is z-scored per row over the names where that member is finite, and the cell averages sign_k * z_k over the members present, NaN when fewer than `min_members` are present. This is a combining rule, not filling (`no_fill` holds: no value is invented, the average runs over what exists). For the ridge, training stays complete-case (its moments need it); with `min_members` set, prediction at a cell sums b_k * z_k over the members present when at least `min_members` are present, NaN otherwise, documented in the docstring as the same rule. Evidence gains `member_coverage` (distribution of present-member counts over scored rows) only when the field is set.
2. In-fold signs (R-187-17). For a family with no stated prior (corpus_family, generated_family) the equal-weight sign comes from training folds. Design 4.2 says "expanding training-fold IC"; design 4.5 requires every fitted part's training reach to enter L, and an expanding fold has unbounded reach, which would push L to the whole history and empty the E17 test. So the fold is trailing with a pinned `sign_window_sessions`, refit every `sign_refit_sessions` (anchored per rule 3 when `refit_anchor` is set), with the ridge's embargo (fwd_span(horizon)) between the last training row and the refit row. The IC is the mean over training rows of the per-row cross-sectional correlation between the member's z-score and the S1 target. A zero or undefined fold IC makes the member absent for that block (handled by rule 1; without min_members the cell is NaN, as complete-case requires). training_rows = window + embargo, so book_memory() picks it up. This trailing-fold choice is recorded in the methodology-change ledger under E17 with its reason.
3. Calendar-anchored refits (R-187-18). `refit_positions` anchors to row 0 today, so a forward run starting on another row refits on other dates. With `refit_anchor` (ISO date) set, the refit grid is the business days d with `np.busday_count(anchor, d) % refit_sessions == 0`, and each grid date maps to the first panel row of the first panel session on or after it (holidays never skip a refit), keeping the first-refit rule that the window must be full.

Spec values (min_members, sign_window_sessions, sign_refit_sessions, refit_anchor) are pinned in the spec like the ridge fields, never read from APR at run time; no APR keys.

  a missing session, never interpolated.

  memory rule makes an expanding fold's reach the whole history and leaves E17 nothing to score.
  The plan records this in the methodology-change ledger and flags it for the owner in
  187-REVIEWS.md; if the owner rules for expanding, the rule ships refused under E17 instead.

  `sign_window_sessions`/`sign_refit_sessions` exactly when some sign is "in_fold", and signs
  accept "in_fold"; existing specs validate unchanged (pinned-hash test).
- (AGY) Ridge on a row with missing members: the forecast over present members is rescaled by
  sqrt(b' S b / b_p' S_p b_p) from the training fold's member covariance S, so a missing member
  does not shrink that name's rank toward the median; a test checks the cross-sectional variance
  of the forecast is unchanged in expectation when a member drops out at random.
