<objective>
Pod books (design 4.1, todo 442 item 1): families on different clocks combine only at the P&L level. A pod's series is the session-level sum of its member books' D_s series, and it is its own book type and attempt. This makes attempt 1c of design 6.1 runnable once 1a and 1b exist.

What this plan does not do: it authors no real book or pod spec and runs nothing on the real vintage. Writing the 1a, 1b and 1c specs is a pre-registration act, and build-first holds except for the one owner-approved family 2 evidence run (187-RESEARCH.md). Runnability is proven in synthetic mode with a fake ledger and in the integration harness.

Statistic: `hac.hac_mean_test` on the pod series, the same decision statistic each member book used (E17), reported with the annualized Sharpe of the series. Tested sessions are those where every member book has a finite D_s (intersection, disclosed as counts per member); no session is filled. Power: a pod adds no fitted part and no new data; each member book already passed its own E16 (d) power refusal before producing its series, so the pod has no separate power plant. This is stated in pod.py's docstring and in the evidence record (`power: inherited`).
