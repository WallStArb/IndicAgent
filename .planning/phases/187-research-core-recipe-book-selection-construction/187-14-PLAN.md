<objective>
Contribution accounting as a compute-only S8 extension (design 13; R-187-19). Two questions with different measures: who earned the return (attribution, additive) and who is necessary (marginal, counterfactual). Diagnostics, not tests: per-member numbers are never significance claims, and they never edit the book they measure (a pruned book is a new version and a new attempt).

Exact identities:
- Forecast level, any construction: with book_forecast[t, i] = sum_k v_k[t, i] * s_k[t, i] (v_k the per-cell member weight the combiner actually applied, including the missing-member average of plan 09 and the ridge's per-fold b/sd), member k's term is sum over scored cells of v_k s_k (r - rbar_t) / N, and the terms sum to the forecast's covariance with the target exactly (test to 1e-12 relative). This needs every combiner to expose its applied weights: add `Combiner.member_weights(stack, target, *, row_session_dates=None) -> [n, m, K]` with the identity combine == sum_k weights * stack checked in tests.
- P&L level, exact for linear construction rules (vol_normalized, mean_variance, fixed_sign): walk-forward position weight x member share; under R1 (rank) member terms do not add up to book P&L, so the P&L split is by family Shapley values, which sum to the total by construction (design 13 item 1).
- Leave-one-family-out: refit the book without family k with the book's own combiner on its own folds (ridge closed form or equal-weight subset), same construction and horizon rule, E17 statistic; report the change in t and Sharpe.
- Shapley over families when the book has 4 or more: exact enumeration up to 12 families, seeded permutation sampling beyond (APR keys `alpha.research.contribution.shapley_exact_max_families` = 12 [conventional], `.shapley_samples`, `.shapley_seed`; changing the seed changes outputs, noted in the description).
- Uniqueness: per member, 1 - R^2 of its z-score on the other members' (pooled over scored cells). Effective breadth: (sum lambda)^2 / sum lambda^2 of the member correlation matrix. Weight-sign stability: fraction of folds where each member's fitted weight keeps its modal sign (ridge, in-fold signs).
- Accounting groups (design 13): family for prior-driven families; feature origin from the member recipe's code_pointer module (plan 11) plus in-fold correlation clusters (hierarchical, computed on each training fold only, never full-vintage, threshold APR `alpha.research.contribution.cluster_corr` [initial_estimate]) inside corpus or generated families.

Counting: the glossary's `attempt` includes "a diagnostic subset refit". Each subset refit whose statistic is computed on the vintage is recorded through S6 as kind `subset_refit` (new CHECK value), same run_group and mode as its book, spec_hash = sha256(book spec_hash + sorted subset), series stored, excluded from the spec-once refusal; StepM prices its correlation with the parent. A single-family book has no family subsets and records none.

Typed headline columns (design 10.2.3, 13): `n_families int`, `effective_breadth float8`, `top_group_share float8` (largest attribution share), `lofo_max_t_drop float8`, `sign_stability_min float8`; NULL at insert, once-only at the terminal update; the research_run guard function is replaced to include them.
