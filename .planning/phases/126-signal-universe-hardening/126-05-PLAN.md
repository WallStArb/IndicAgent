<objective>
Run a two-layer signal quality audit (Root Crime statistical layer). Layer 1: per-plugin Information Coefficient (IC = correlation between pre_quality_confidence and realized pnl_r), hit_rate, and bootstrap 95% CI for every plugin with >= 30 outcomes in signal_ledger. Layer 2: detection-condition verifiability — for VALIDATED and NOISE-CANDIDATE plugins, sample signals and check whether the plugin's claimed detection-condition feature fields are actually populated at signal time.

Purpose: Per Renaissance principles, a plugin's claim to detect a pattern is credible only if mechanically verifiable from features at signal time, and statistically validated by predictive power. Anti-signal plugins (IC < -0.02 or hit_rate CI upper < 0.45) and unverifiable plugins are demoted to shadow_only=True with documented rationale. They stay in TIER_I7 — every firing is training data (D-12).

Output: production/scripts/signal_quality_audit.py (two layers), docs/plans/2026-06-14-phase-126-signal-quality-audit-results.md (IC league table + detection verifiability table + dispositions applied).

DEPENDS ON Wave 2 (P126-02): the audit reflects plugins after the exempt-set deletion and MeanReversion/FVGFill dispositions, so it runs after Wave 2 completes.
</objective>
