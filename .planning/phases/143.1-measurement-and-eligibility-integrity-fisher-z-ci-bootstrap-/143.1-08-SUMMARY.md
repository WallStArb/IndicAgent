# Phase 143.1 Plan 08: Shadow-Mode Champion/Challenger Validation Summary

**HOLD — Component E's sign-symmetric eligibility flag does not clear its pre-committed
promotion bar this round. `alpha.ensemble.sign_symmetric` stays `false`. The eligibility
mechanism itself works exactly as designed (challenger admits a genuine, large short-side
population), but the OOS P&L result for what it admits is net-negative and fails 3 of the
4 gating criteria decisively.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~2h20min end-to-end (counterfactual_tracker backfill dominates: 3 failed
  attempts totaling ~18h wall-clock before a real throughput bug was found and fixed, then
  ~78min for the corrected full-corpus run)
- **Completed:** 2026-07-21

## What Happened

1. Verified `143.1-08-SHADOW-VALIDATION.md` Sections 1-3a (pre-committed decision rule) were
   already correctly committed to git before any result-producing command ran, per this
   phase's own goalpost-integrity requirement.
2. Confirmed champion (`--no-sign-symmetric`) and challenger (`--sign-symmetric`)
   `ensemble_trainer` runs, `alpha_publisher --skip-kafka` scoring, and `alpha_frame_writer
   --backfill` had already completed (1,153,234 champion / 22,009,156 challenger `alpha_frames`
   rows) — but `counterfactual_tracker --backfill` had not: 100% of all 23.16M `alpha_frames`
   rows across every `weight_epoch` were still `status='open'`, across 3 separate attempts.
3. Diagnosed the root cause (not assumed): `_execute_inner`'s `exe.map(...)` yields results in
   *submission order*, not completion order. A slow head-of-line symbol/tf partition blocked
   every faster worker's already-completed result from flushing, and with no per-symbol
   checkpoint, every restart discarded prior progress and re-ran the same slow partition from
   zero — a self-repeating zero-progress loop across ~18 hours, not "it's just slow." A deeper
   profiling pass found the actual per-row cost: 29 rows/sec via the hypertable vs. 10,423
   rows/sec writing directly to the resolved `_timescaledb_internal` chunk (358x) — TimescaleDB's
   per-execution chunk-routing overhead against 1,034 chunks, paid on every row regardless of
   batching. Fixed (chunk resolved once per run via binary search, direct chunk writes with a
   safe hypertable fallback) and tracked as this worktree's own completed todo 161.
4. Re-ran the corrected `counterfactual_tracker --backfill`: completed successfully in ~78 min,
   23,151,500 rows written, only 890 frames left open (too recent to resolve, expected).
5. Wrote `scripts/analysis/phase143_1_08_shadow_validation.py`, reusing `frame_gate_passes()`
   verbatim (per Section 3's pre-commitment) to compute criteria 1/2/3/4/6/7 for both
   `weight_epoch`s.
6. Before trusting the resulting Sharpe (-4.14) and max-drawdown (360,733%) numbers, sanity-
   checked the underlying row-level data rather than accepting extreme statistics at face
   value: found `counterfactual_pnl_r` has a long tail to -57.27R, traced to
   `compute_frame_geometry()`'s ATR-guard only catching `atr<=0`, not small-but-legitimate ATR
   on thin-absolute-volatility instruments producing razor-thin (0.02%-0.3% of price) stop
   distances. Verified this does NOT change the qualitative conclusion (trimmed-outlier check:
   both champion and challenger remain net-negative) before filing it as an independent
   follow-up (todo 162) rather than letting it either invalidate or get silently absorbed into
   this shadow validation's own verdict.
7. Appended Section 6 to `143.1-08-SHADOW-VALIDATION.md` with the full criteria table, the
   HOLD verdict, the emission-volume re-measurement (Section 5, folded in), and the todo-162
   caveat, without editing Sections 1-5/3a.
