<objective>
Build the novelty dimension (FIT-02) and the geometric-mean composite combiner (D-04, D-05). Novelty is the only population-level dimension: for a target agent it computes `1 - max(|Pearson r|)` against every other live agent over their overlapping resolved `signal_id` sets (the join key is the resolved signal_id), measuring PnL decorrelation (true alpha redundancy, not analytical agreement). Composite is the geometric mean of all 5 dimensions with the hard rule that any NULL dimension makes composite NULL and any 0.0 dimension collapses composite to 0.0.

Purpose: Novelty must be a function over the full population (RESEARCH pitfall 4 — cannot be computed per-agent in isolation). The composite combiner is the structural enforcement of "weaknesses cannot be hidden" — geometric mean, no weighted-linear alternative, no compensation logic.
Output: novelty.py, composite.py, and a shared test file.
</objective>
