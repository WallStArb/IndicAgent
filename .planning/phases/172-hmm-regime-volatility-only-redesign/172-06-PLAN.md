<objective>
Repoint `ic_engine.py`'s per-symbol regime stratification from `feature_vectors.regime` to
`feature_vectors.regime_volatility`, and produce a written audit of the three adjacent pieces of
regime machinery that must not change.

Purpose: `172-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 2 is explicit that "swap regime for regime_volatility" is not a
one-line rename. Four semi-independent things thread through this file under the same word:
the crash-loud startup gate, the per-symbol regime source and its cross-sectional fallback, the
`alpha.regime.groups` JSON-APR routing with its per-group `dual_write_symbol_hmm` field, and the
`_POOLED_REGIME_SENTINEL`. Only the first two are actually being cut over. The other two belong to
the systematic `market_regimes` system, which `171-FINAL-VERDICT.md` does not retire, and the point
of giving them their own task is to prove that in the codebase rather than assert it from a
reading of the verdict.

One decision this plan makes and records: `feature_ic_scores.regime_scope` does not get a new
enum value. `symbol_hmm` accurately names the label SOURCE, which is still a per-symbol
`GaussianHMM`, and the two label vocabularies are disjoint sets of strings, so the `regime` column
alone distinguishes a trend-vintage row from a volatility-vintage row. Adding a fourth scope value
would ripple into the `AND regime_scope = %(pass_type)s` filters the shrinkage and promotion
queries bind, for no discriminating power the label strings do not already provide. What does need
changing is the column's COMMENT, which currently states the scope means "5 per-symbol HMM labels
from feature_vectors.regime" and becomes actively wrong the moment this cutover ships.

Output: two repointed code paths, one comment-correcting migration, and one audit document.
</objective>
