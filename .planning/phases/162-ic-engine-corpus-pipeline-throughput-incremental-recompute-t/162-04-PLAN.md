Prove empirically that the fingerprint captures everything that determines a cell's IC: run a
~5-symbol subset twice — once forced fresh (`--refresh`), once via the fingerprint-skip path — and
assert byte/value-identical `feature_ic_scores` including post-backfill `bh_adjusted_p`/`passes_fdr`.

cross-run instead of intra-run: the harness is the third leg of the defense-in-depth (watermarks +
field-classification test + this).

give, and lay down the staleness policy — a fingerprint-valid cell is never auto-stale; data-driven
refresh is an explicit `--training-window-end` act. Seed the drift-tolerance key disabled until an
empirical study justifies otherwise.

migration 251 seeding `alpha.ic.refresh_min_new_fraction` at 0; a bound read of that key in
