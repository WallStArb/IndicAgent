<objective>
Build the Theory-Motivated Interaction Layer proper: 10 curated compound features, each with a
stated finance-theory hypothesis, each a single operation on two numeric tier-0 columns.

Purpose: ROADMAP's design rules for this phase are explicit — a curated layer, NOT a combinatorial
factory. ~30K compound candidates in a BH-FDR pool at FDR=0.05 produces ~1,500 expected false
discoveries and destroys the correction's power guarantees; that design (deferred todo 019) was
rejected on statistical-power grounds before this phase existed. The ≤50 cap is the Musk-step-3
"simplify" already applied at design time, not a scope limitation. Combined with plan 151-05's 5
named candidates and the 8 pre-existing live rows, this brings the tier-1 population to 23 — well
inside the cap, and sized so BH-FDR retains real power.

**Resolution of `151-RESEARCH.md` Open Question 2 (categorical-regime encoding), decided here:**
ROADMAP's candidate-source list names "momentum × low_vol_regime" and "mean-reversion × regime
label," but `market_regimes` and `feature_vectors.regime` are categorical STRINGS, not numeric
`FeatureVector` columns — a product needs two numeric operands. Decision: option (a) from
RESEARCH.md, numeric-proxy substitution. Every "× regime" candidate uses an existing numeric tier-0
regime proxy (`hv_ratio` for volatility expansion/contraction, `adx` for trend strength, `hurst`
and `variance_ratio_fast` for persistence/mean-reversion, `vix_z` for market-wide volatility,
`yield_slope_z` for term-structure regime). Rationale: this keeps every compound a persisted
numeric column that `partial_spearman_ic`'s 2-control contract can screen directly, and avoids
opening a second stratification surface alongside the one `ic_engine` already owns.

Output: 10 new `FeatureVector` fields, 10 `feature_vectors` columns, 10 `feature_registry` rows
(`tier='1_interaction'`), a documented redundancy check, unit coverage. No new APR keys — every
compound is parameterless by the single-operation design rule.
</objective>
