<objective>
Assemble the single IBKR history fetcher (CD-01, CD-02, CD-07): a BaseBatch oneshot that takes
the singleton lock, walks the priority queue item by item within a time budget, records every
outcome in the ledger, promotes the grid in the same pass, and is killable from outside when
in-process timers fail. Then dry-run its ranking against live state and compare it with
today's order (CD-10) before anything is allowed to touch IBKR in production.

Purpose: this is the one process that will own the IBKR history connection after cutover.
Output: `ibkr_history_fetcher.py`, systemd unit files in the repo (not installed; plan 06
installs them), service registry entries, tests, and a recorded dry-run comparison.
</objective>
