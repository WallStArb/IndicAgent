<objective>
Build the SCALAR candidate (D-01b, D-03.1): extend `EnsembleICEngine` with a `_calibrate_stop_target()`
method that derives per-(regime,tf) `stop_atr_mult` and `target_r_multiple` APR values empirically,
mirroring the EXACT structure of the existing `_calibrate_hold_max_bars()` (grouping →
per-symbol selection → median across qualifying symbols → CR-02 champion gate → skip-if-empty →
ConfigService.set), but with a NEW, stop/target-appropriate selection criterion (RESEARCH
Finding 1 / Pitfall 1 — the IC-decay-walk does NOT transfer).

Purpose: `stop_atr_mult=1.5`/`target_r_multiple=2.0` are single global `[initial_estimate]`
scalars never conditioned on regime/tf (D-02) — the real gap Phase 148's Gate 2 FAIL exposed.
This is one of the two competing candidates D-03 requires; the winner is decided by the gate
(Plan 166-06), not by inspection.

Output: `_calibrate_stop_target()` + a pure `_select_stop_target_from_excursions()` selection
function, wired into the champion-gate dispatch alongside `_calibrate_hold_max_bars()`, and unit
tests.

LOCKED DESIGN DECISION (resolves RESEARCH Open Question 2 — stated explicitly per Renaissance
rigor): the selection criterion is a percentile of the naturally-uncensored excursion
subpopulation, NOT a grid-search re-simulation and NOT a naive percentile over all frames.
- stop_atr_mult := `alpha.ensemble_ic.stop_mae_percentile` (default 90th) of ATR-rescaled
  `counterfactual_mae` among `counterfactual_status='closed_target'` frames (winners never touched
  the stop → their MAE is uncensored w.r.t. the stop; a stop tighter than this percentile would
  have prematurely clipped winners).
- target_r_multiple := `alpha.ensemble_ic.target_mfe_percentile` (default 50th) of R-unit
  `counterfactual_mfe` among `counterfactual_status='closed_max_hold'` frames (time-exit frames
  never hit the target → their MFE is uncensored w.r.t. the target).
This sidesteps todo 088's right-censoring bias by construction. The disclosure of the residual
selection assumption goes in the ConfigService.set `reason` string.
</objective>
