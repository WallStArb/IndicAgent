<objective>
Amended 2026-10-06 by the data layer integrity design (`docs/plans/2026-10-06-data-layer-integrity-design.md`
sections 6 and 9 step 4, owner-approved). Was: four D7 facts plus a content-aware check_d2_landed.
Now: `lineage_value_mismatch` becomes `lineage_missing` read from the 185-38 lineage view, and the scope
grows to the full computed verdict report, 1d half. The intraday half (slot_coverage, intraday
digest_fresh, coverage_cache, grid_parity, stray_vendor_rows) is 185-40; the gates that read the
verdicts (promote script, rebuild_preconditions, the live D2 test, alert rules, the 186 note) are 185-41.
`mixed_source_names` is dropped: the source policy now decides every name's source and
policy_conformance checks it.

Design: the expensive truth (recompute d2-v2 and every 1d digest over about 7M bars) runs once a night
in D7, which already has its own timer (189-06, 06:00 UTC), and lands as verdict rows. Gates read rows
plus their age, so they stay cheap and read-only. Pure arithmetic lives in src/intelligence/bars/
(vendor_basis.py, integrity_checks.py); D7 does IO. Recomputation reuses `derive_daily_v2` (185-36) and
the digest arithmetic (`bar_content_digest`, `month_ranges` in src/intelligence/bars/digest.py), never a
copy. Symbol by symbol, never a full-corpus wide frame (CLAUDE.md).

lineage_missing reads the view when 185-38 measured its full-corpus count under 10 minutes; otherwise it
uses the equivalent set-based join (same predicate) and the SUMMARY says which.

Check rules (spec section 6 table):
- session_coverage (1d): (bars plus answered-empty sessions) over NYSE sessions since the name's first
  bar, threshold `threshold.bar_integrity.session_coverage_min_1d` 0.999.
- policy_conformance: every visible 1d row's source matches the open policy row for its date, or is
  ibkr_fallback on a date with no Tradier observation; zero.
- canonical_recompute and digest_fresh: zero.
- vendor_basis_run: reported always; blocks only when the canonical side is discontinuous.
- report_age: the name's newest verdict is newer than its latest ohlcv_load row, within
  `threshold.bar_integrity.report_max_age_hours` 30; D7 reads the key at init and writes the verdict, and
  the gates (185-41) re-evaluate age against now.

Migration 449 seeds only the keys this plan reads (keys are seeded where they are first read, so the
185-44 reader guard stays green): session_coverage_min_1d, vendor_ratio_run_min_sessions and
report_max_age_hours, all read by D7 at init. 185-40 seeds its own two keys. Re-check the number is
free; apply and commit together. Unit tests use fakes (todo 494). No em dashes; no AI attribution.
</objective>
