<objective>
Repair the corpus the pre-185-27 loader left behind (VERIFICATION gap 1's data half and gap 2's
flag cleanup): lineage and digests for every Tradier-owned name, and retirement of the legacy
price-sanity flags that now hide clean Tradier bars.

Why retire rather than keep: a legacy_price_sanity_status flag (migration 381, 15 rows, all
confirmed_corrupt) describes the IBKR value it was judged on. Where the Tradier load replaced that
value, the judged value lives on in ohlcv_revision and the stored bar is a different observation
(SPY 2007-04-02 is now high 142.46 close 142.16). The stored Tradier bar was scrubbed by every D2a
rule on 2026-10-03; a bar a rule still flags (EFA 2008-09-29 and XRT 2008-09-19 carry other flags)
stays quarantined by that rule. Flags are not raw data; the raw values are permanent in D1 and
ohlcv_revision, and the retirement is recorded.

Performance: about 6.3M lineage upserts against a non-hypertable side table plus about 1,266 digest
passes. 185-18 wrote 3.88M lineage rows in a 200 s apply. Follow
docs/foundation/performance-investigation-sop.md: time 10 names first, extrapolate, then run all;
per-symbol transactions; one serial connection (no worker writes). The script is not imported by
the HTF lane and takes no IBKR lease. Unit tests use fakes (todo 494). No em dashes; no AI
attribution.
</objective>
