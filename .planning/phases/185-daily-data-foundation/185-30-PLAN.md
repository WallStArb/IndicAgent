<objective>
Repair the 1d corpus the pre-185-27 loader and digest ordering left behind (VERIFICATION gap 1's
data half and gap 2's flag cleanup): lineage for every Tradier-owned name, current digests for every
canonical 1d name, the 31 untraced IBKR bars, and retirement of the 1d legacy price-sanity flags
that now hide clean Tradier bars.

Why retire rather than keep: a legacy_price_sanity_status flag (migration 381) describes the IBKR
value it was judged on. Where the Tradier load replaced that value, the judged value lives on in
ohlcv_revision and the stored bar is a different observation (SPY 2007-04-02 is now high 142.46
close 142.16). The stored Tradier bar was scrubbed by every D2a rule on 2026-10-03; a bar a rule
still flags (EFA 2008-09-29 and XRT 2008-09-19 carry other flags) stays quarantined by that rule.
Flags are not raw data; raw values are permanent in D1 and ohlcv_revision, and the retirement is
recorded. Only timeframe 1d flags are in scope: 567 legacy rows exist across timeframes and the
552 intraday ones are never touched.

Untraced IBKR bars (orchestrator call 2026-10-06, pending owner review): 31 stored IBKR 1d bars
have neither lineage nor any D1 observation. Re-ask IBKR for those dates under verify-only and
re-derive; whatever stays unanswered remains quarantined and is counted by 185-33 as
untraced_quarantined_1d, a fact that does not fail check_d2_landed or lineage_value_mismatch
because quarantined bars are not visible to research.

Performance: about 6.3M lineage upserts against a non-hypertable side table plus a digest pass per
canonical 1d name (about 1,450). 185-18 wrote 3.88M lineage rows in a 200 s apply. Follow
docs/foundation/performance-investigation-sop.md: time 10 names first, extrapolate, then run all;
per-symbol transactions; one serial connection (no worker writes); `VACUUM ANALYZE
canonical_bar_lineage` after the roughly 3.18M-row update. The script is not imported by the HTF
lane. Task 3's re-ask takes the ibkr_history_stream lease at priority tier (client 49, D-30) through
the historical pipeline, which is NOT edited. Unit tests use fakes (todo 494). No em dashes; no AI
attribution.
