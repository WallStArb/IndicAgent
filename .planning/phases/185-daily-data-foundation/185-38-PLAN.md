<objective>
Spec step 3, second half, and step 9 (`docs/plans/2026-10-06-data-layer-integrity-design.md` sections 4
and 9, owner-approved 2026-10-06): one 1d writer, lineage derived on read, and the live re-derivation of
every name under d2-v2 (built and measured in 185-36). Supersedes 185-27's loader lineage writes and
rule tradier-v1 and 185-30's lineage rows (both stay in history; their D1 elision, scrub and digest
repairs stand).

Loader decisions (spec section 4: "keeps its fetch, D1 elision and refusal logic, writes only D1 and
ohlcv_load, then chains the daily stage"):
- Canonical write, lineage upsert, unmatched check, `LineageGapError`, `_scrub_and_digest` and the
  `--raw-only` flag are deleted: every run is raw.
- Refusal keeps its meaning on the raw record: a load whose Tradier answer revises more than
  `threshold.bar_integrity.max_revision_ratio` of the name's latest Tradier D1 observations is refused
  (outcome gated, nothing landed) unless the loader inferred a split from it (it still writes the
  `tradier_refetch` corporate_action row in the same transaction). It stops reading
  `infra.tradier.max_changed_bar_ratio` (retired by 185-43).
- `short_history` stops being a refusal. It was an ownership rule, and ownership now lives in
  bar_source_policy; refusing it would leave the 236 names' Tradier series stale while the policy names
  Tradier primary. The load lands D1 and records outcome `loaded` with `short_history` in detail.
- After the loop, one subprocess `services/bar_derivation.py --stage daily --symbols <changed> --apply`
  for the names with landed observations or an inferred split; its exit code joins the loader's.

`tradier_owned` contract with phase 189: the fetcher (`ibkr_history_fetcher.py`, not edited here)
skips IBKR 1d for names where `_SELECT_DAILY_CHANGED_SINCE_SQL.tradier_owned` is true. Redefine the
column as "the open 1d policy row for the name has primary_source tradier and a TRADIER observation
exists", so the stopped fetcher keeps skipping the same names until 189-10 replaces this probe with the
weekly IBKR 1d reconcile.

Rollback (no destructive live step without one):
- Lineage: `pg_dump -Fc -t canonical_bar_lineage` before the drop, kept 30 days in data/backups/185-38/
  (gitignored /data/). Restore = drop view, pg_restore the table.
- Canonical 1d: a `\copy` snapshot of every 1d row before the re-derivation; `bar_derivation.py
  --stage daily --restore-snapshot <file> --symbols <list> --apply` rewrites those symbols' 1d rows from
  it through the same write contract (old values to ohlcv_revision, caller bar_derivation-restore), then
  scrubs and digests. The revision rows of the re-derivation give the per-bar record.
- Loader: `git revert` of Task 1's commit restores tradier-v1 writes (only meaningful together with the
  lineage restore).

Live-window rules: run Task 3 outside 00:30-03:00 UTC (the 01:30 UTC Tradier timer) and before 05:30 UTC
or after 06:30 UTC (the D7 timer); stop `indicagent-tradier-daily.timer` for the duration and start it
again at the end (rollback of that step: start it). The fetcher service and timer stay stopped (owner
decision 2026-10-06). The re-derivation rewrites 1d rows across most of `market_data_ohlcv`'s compressed
chunks: follow `docs/foundation/performance-investigation-sop.md`, measure the canary's write rate, and
if it is slow, decompress the touched chunks first and recompress after, never theorize.

Migration 447: re-check free, apply and commit together. Unit tests use fakes (todo 494). No em dashes;
