stays the second 1d writer (owner decision 2026-10-03, not re-litigated) but its write path now
does what D2 does for IBKR names: lineage to the raw D1 observation under a versioned rule
(tradier-v1), the D2a scrub, and fresh content digests. It also stops the nightly full-history
refetch from appending the whole history to D1 every night (about 6.3M rows a night, 2.3B a year;
found while planning: `_land_in_d1` lands every fetched bar and `ohlcv_observation`'s key is
(request_id, bar_date)), and stops upserting every fetched bar into `market_data_ohlcv` (109 of
110 chunks are compressed; `_record_load` today rewrites about 6.3M unchanged rows a night, each
batch decompressing its chunk and leaving dead heap pages). The plan writes only new and changed
bars, which `plan_symbol_load` already computes (n_new, plan.revisions).

(the loader's `_SELECT_EXISTING_SQL`, `_SELECT_MISSING_SQL`, `_SELECT_NIGHTLY_SQL`; D2's comments

rows since 185-25.
