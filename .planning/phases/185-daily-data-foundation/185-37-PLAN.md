<objective>
Spec step 5 (`docs/plans/2026-10-06-data-layer-integrity-design.md` sections 2, 7 and open risks;
owner-approved 2026-10-06): measure the two vendors' adjustment-basis disagreement on known answers and
write per-name exception policy rows only on evidence. The spec lists the vendor treatment of IBM, BDX
and LEN as unverified; this plan verifies it.

Pre-registered decision rule (write it into the doc and commit before running the study):
1. A run is a span of at least `threshold.bar_integrity.vendor_ratio_run_min_sessions` sessions where
   the IBKR/Tradier close ratio leaves `threshold.bar_integrity.fallback_basis_tolerance_bp`.
2. At each run boundary, the continuous vendor is the one whose own close-to-close move at the boundary
   is within the name's ordinary range (|log return| below the name's 99th percentile daily move over the
   surrounding 250 sessions) while the other vendor's move is not; a recorded corporate_action on the
   boundary date explains a step on the vendor that applies it.
3. Tradier stays primary unless Tradier is the discontinuous side with no corporate action explaining
   it and IBKR is continuous; then an exception row sets primary_source ibkr for the run's date range
   (valid_from and valid_to bound the range), evidence = the run (dates, median ratio, both boundary
   moves).
4. Spin-offs: the canonical series should not show a false return at the ex-date. If Tradier adjusts
   history for the spin-off and IBKR does not, Tradier is already right and no row is written; if the
   reverse, an exception row applies. Undecided runs get no row and are listed by name.
The 99th percentile and 250 sessions are study parameters recorded in the doc, not APR keys (the study
is a one-off measurement; the daily rule's thresholds are the APR keys above).

Policy writer: `scripts/ops/bars/ops_source_policy.py --add --symbol S --timeframe 1d --valid-from D
--valid-to D --primary ibkr --reason "..." --evidence <json file>` and `--close --symbol S --timeframe 1d
--as-of D`; dry run by default, `--apply` writes; it refuses a row without evidence or one that
overlaps an open row (the exclusion constraint would also refuse). Rollback of a row is a `--close`
(append-only table, spec section 1).

The study script is research tooling under scripts/research/ (read-only on the database). Unit tests
use fakes (todo 494). No em dashes; no AI attribution.
</objective>
