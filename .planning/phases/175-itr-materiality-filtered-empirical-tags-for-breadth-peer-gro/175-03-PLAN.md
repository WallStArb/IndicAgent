<objective>
Add Pass 4 to `TagCalibrator`: compute and persist the orthogonalized materiality evidence for
every kept empirical `(symbol, tag)` pair, and promote `discovery_state`/`first_measured_at`
out of the `evidence` JSONB into the typed columns migration 346 created (todo 125).

Purpose: D-01 puts the measurement in exactly one place. This is that place. The evidence is
written **unconditionally** for every kept empirical row - it is a measurement, not an
admission decision (D-01). Nothing in this plan changes any consumer's behavior.

**Measurement universe (D-01a, amended 2026-09-18 by the D-07 review):** "kept" means the row
clears Pass 1-3's existing gate, `passes_fdr AND abs(loading) >= loading_threshold`. D-01's
original text said "every `source='empirical'` row"; the cross-AI review (175-REVIEWS.md,
CONFIRMED) found that wider than what this plan implements, and D-01 was amended to the
narrower scope rather than the plan widened. Reasons, recorded in 175-CONTEXT.md D-01a: a row
Pass 1-3 already rejected is not an admission candidate; measuring it could produce
`passes_fdr = false` beside `passes_materiality = true` on the same row, an internally
contradictory evidence set; and the null-arm Monte Carlo is not free. Non-kept rows get
`passes_materiality = false` by construction through
`_UPDATE_FAILING_OR_EXPIRE_EMPIRICAL_SQL` (Task 3b), never NULL-and-ambiguous. Within the kept
population the evidence is still written unconditionally - measurement and admission stay
separate decisions.
Output: a 4-pass `TagCalibrator` whose next run populates the eleven new `instrument_tags`
columns.

**Shadow-mode boundary (D-03, non-negotiable):** this plan does NOT touch
`src/intelligence/regime_signals/breadth_vol.py` or
`services/cross_sectional_regime_model.py`. Both keep querying `source = 'human'` exactly as

**Planner resolutions carried by this plan** (subject to the D-07 cross-AI review gate):

- **R-02 (RESEARCH.md Open Q2 - inline vs. offline null arm):** the D-06 circular-shift null
  runs **inline in Pass 4**, writing `null_arm_p_value` / `null_arm_bh_p` every run. This
  overrides RESEARCH.md's tentative "put it in the offline script" recommendation, for three
  reasons: (a) P175-03 requires the shadow diagnostic to be strictly read-only, and a
  "read-only script that writes one column back" is a contradiction that would also break the
  grep gate 175-VALIDATION.md specifies; (b) D-01 states the evidence columns are written by
  `TagCalibrator` unconditionally, and `null_arm_p_value` is evidence; (c) plan 02's kernel
  precomputes the control pseudo-inverse and the candidate residual once per pair, so each
  draw costs one matmul rather than an `lstsq` - the corpus-wide cost is bounded and small for
  a oneshot batch job with no timer.
- **R-07 (Pass 4 lookback):** Pass 4 uses the symbol's **full available daily history**, not
  `tag_vocabulary.lookback_days` (252) as Pass 1 does. Codex's `min_sample_n=756` floor and
  the 4×252-day sign-stability windows cannot fit inside a 252-bar window; the 252-day
  `lookback_days` is Pass 1's per-tag measurement window, while Pass 4 is deliberately a
  longer-horizon materiality judgment. This is also RESEARCH.md Pitfall 2's point: `k=4`
  controls make Pass 4 a structurally different sample-size problem from Pass 1's `k=0`
  bivariate measurement, which is why it gets its own `min_sample_n` key rather than reusing
  `alpha.tag_calibrator.min_sample_n` (60).

  **Known cost of this choice, documented not fixed here:** full-history sign-stability
  windows correctly distrust a short track record, but this means a symbol whose underlying
  business genuinely changed (not the market regime -- the instrument's own asset
  composition, e.g. a software company that becomes a leveraged crypto-treasury vehicle) can
  take years to accumulate enough sign-stable windows even when its CURRENT sensitivity is
  completely real, because earlier-era history (when the relationship legitimately did not
  exist) dilutes and can outright fail the 3-of-4 gate. This is a different failure mode from
  D-06's null-arm (distinguishes signal from noise) and from TAG-02's regime-conditioning
  (same instrument, same business, beta legitimately varies with regime) -- this is a
  company-level structural break, not a market regime. The gate is doing its job (skepticism
  of a short record is correct), but the cost is real and not addressed by anything in this
  phase. See `docs/ideas/itr-extension-opportunities.md` #6 for the candidate fix
  (changepoint-aware history truncation, distinct from TAG-02).

  **Sign-stability is a sliding bar, deliberately (resolved 2026-09-18, D-07 review).** The
  gate `decide_materiality` applies is `sign_stable_windows >= min_sign_stable_windows AND
  sign_stable_windows_total >= min_sign_stable_windows` (both defaulting to 3), not a literal
  "3 of 4". Codex flagged the difference as a gap; AGY assessed the same formulation as sound.
  Independent analysis resolved it: `min_sample_n = 756` is exactly `3 x 252`, so the sample
  floor already guarantees only three full `sign_stability_window_days` windows, not four. A
  symbol sitting at that floor therefore has `sign_stable_windows_total == 3` and must show
  100% sign agreement (3 of 3) to pass, while a symbol with a full four evaluable windows
  needs only 75% (3 of 4) and may carry one disagreement. Shorter-history symbols face a
  STRICTER agreement ratio, not a looser one. That is the opposite of a loophole, and it is
  retained deliberately rather than "fixed":
  - it falls out of the geometry of `min_sample_n` and `sign_stability_window_days`, not from
    an oversight. Tightening the gate to require `sign_stable_windows_total ==
    sign_stability_window_count` would make the `min_sample_n` floor unreachable: 756
    observations can never produce four windows of 252.
  - this is a shadow-mode measurement phase (D-02/D-03). Nothing downstream is gated by
    `passes_materiality` yet, so there is no basis to loosen or tighten a threshold on
    intuition rather than evidence.
  - D-04/D-05 already locked the standing discipline for exactly this situation: seed
    conservative, do not average or guess, recalibrate later through
    `docs/foundation/apr-calibration-backlog.md` once real corpus evidence exists.

  The mechanism that protects against this gate silently hiding real signal is visibility, not
  a threshold guess: plan 04's diagnostic reports near-miss granularity for sign-stability
  failures specifically (the `sign_stable_windows`/`sign_stable_windows_total` distribution of
  FAILING candidates, not only passing ones), so a future recalibration decision is made from
  measured data rather than intuition. Do NOT change the gate condition or either default
  while implementing this plan.
- **R-04 (restated, load-bearing here):** `passes_materiality` is the **statistical gate
  only**. `discovery_state` (temporal) and `valid_to` (expiry) are separate columns. The
  canonical read-time conjunction lives in exactly one place - the new
  `is_materiality_eligible()` helper - so the diagnostic (plan 04) and any future consumer
  import it rather than re-deriving the predicate. This follows RESEARCH.md Pitfall 4 over
  175-PATTERNS.md line 132's contradicting restatement.
