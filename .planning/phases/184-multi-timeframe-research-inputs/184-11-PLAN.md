<objective>
B4: disclose each member's IC term structure by lag with its joint shift band as a run record
that feeds nothing, and state the book's kappa (design D2, glossary "IC term structure").

Purpose: the term structure tells the owner how fast a predictor's information decays, which is
what the fixed smoothing menu and kappa are chosen against before a run. Reading it to reshape
the next family version is a counted re-specification (design section 9 row 1), so the record
must be inert: computed and stored, never read by code. RESEARCH Finding 4: it is not
`measure.term_structure`'s cumulative ladder, so the runner calls the statistics function from
184-03.

Output: runner.py and evidence.py edits plus one test file.
</objective>
