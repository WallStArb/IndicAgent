B6 (design D6 and D7 guard 6, rerun on the built S8): show that the runner's decision statistic
holds size on persistent predictors routed through the 184 nodes, and give the feature family
module its battery pointer.

Purpose: a slow, persistent predictor is exactly where a timing test over-rejects (design D6
history), and feature members are the first persistent ones: 52-week percentiles, smoothed
members with 838-session memory. The design text says E16; the runner's statistic is E17
(`timing.timing_test`, memory-lagged cell mean) and `_check_spec_tree` refuses non-E17 specs
(RESEARCH Pitfall 9), so the cells run E17. Pitfall 8: long memories remove a third or more of
the tested span; the report records tested sessions per cell so the family pre-registration can
disclose the power cost.

Output: the battery module, the feature family module, tests, and the committed battery report.
</objective>
