    - Each symbol card should show the full price hero:
      - BID and ASK prices side by side (not just one price)
      - Large last price with green/red colouring vs prevClose
      - Two change rows: "PREV CLOSE" and "SESSION" with ± amount and %

    - IndicatorGrid should show real values for RSI, MACD, ATR, Bollinger Bands

    - Pattern panel: RSI divergence flag, BB squeeze active/inactive, confluence score

    **6. Signal Panel (DASH-07)**
    - Signal panel shows direction (LONG/SHORT), confidence %, entry price, stop loss
    - High-confidence signals (>75%) may trigger the signal banner at top of card
    - TF matrix (row of 1m/5m/15m/1h dots) shows signal direction per timeframe

    **7. Narrative Panel (DASH-07)**
    - Bottom of dashboard shows narrative feed
    - After AI narrative service produces output for 1m signals: narrative text appears
