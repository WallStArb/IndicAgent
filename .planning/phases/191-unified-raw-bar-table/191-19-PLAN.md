## Amendment 2026-10-23 (readiness evidence basis)

The readiness predicate above is unsatisfiable by design for names whose Tradier span is
already settled by evidence: the gap planner never issues a window into covered ground, so no
post-depth-change ask can ever start at or below the threshold for them (review of the plan 19
not-ready record, 2026-10-23: 498 covered-but-blind names, 75 with a verified 1d provider head
above the threshold, 11 with a deep no_data answer window). Readiness settles a name's Tradier
span when ANY of:

- (a) the original ask-row predicate holds: a post-depth-change IBKR 1d ask (ohlcv_request,
  source ibkr, outcome bars or no_data) whose window start is at or before the threshold;
- (b) IBKR holds a 1d raw bar (smart_trades or a `venue_%` route kind) at or before the
  threshold;
- (c) a definitive no_data request window (any age, no open transport failure; the gap
  planner's own `daily_no_data_coverage` definition) starts at or before the threshold;
- (d) a verified 1d `ohlcv_provider_head` for ibkr sits above the threshold (it floors every
  future ask above it).

Freshness-gated `ohlcv_empty_history` spans do NOT settle readiness: they re-open on reverify
and get re-asked, which then satisfies (a). Readiness's scope is unchanged: it guarantees the
deep end of the Tradier span (the only part the 9800-day depth change was about); interior
gaps remain the standing cycle's job and appear in the residue report. Names the owner holds
frozen under todo 519 are excluded from the gate: the hold has no machine-readable surface
(only two of the four names carry `bar_source_policy` rows; no APR key or instruments flag
exists), so the harness takes an explicit `--frozen-names` argument (caller passes the 519
list; default none; no hardcoded symbols in the script) and flags those names in the readiness
output with their measured residue class.
