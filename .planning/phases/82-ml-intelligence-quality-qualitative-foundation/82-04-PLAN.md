<objective>
Replace the binary regime gate with a three-band soft multiplier. The middle band (`REGIME_PROB_MIN <= prob < REGIME_PROB_SOFT_MAX`) keeps signals eligible but linearly attenuates `calibrated_confidence` toward a floor at the low end, scaling back to 1.0 at the upper boundary.

Purpose: Mathematical correction — binary gate at 0.30 loses signal in the high-noise transition window that a soft attenuation could preserve at reduced weight.
Output: Updated gate function + Settings + Prometheus counter + unit tests.

Depends on Plan 02: the soft path reads `hmm_regime_entropy` from the features dict; that field is produced by HMMRegimePlugin only after Plan 02 is merged.
</objective>
