## Decisions Made
- **WEIGHT_EPOCH is boundary identity, not per-run uniqueness.** Two corpus runs sharing the same `TRAINING_WINDOW_END` deliberately share one epoch — `DO UPDATE` makes a same-epoch re-run idempotent (overwrite, not corrupt or collide-and-skip). Full `corpus_runs`/`run_id` lineage remains explicitly out of scope for this plan (deferred hardening item per roadmap).
- **Pure digit-extraction for epoch derivation** (`tr -cd '0-9'` on the freeze timestamp) rather than any arithmetic — no off-by-one surface, deterministic, filesystem/PK-safe.
- Kept the code comment describing the DO UPDATE rationale phrased as "DO UPDATE, never silently no-op" (rather than literally writing "not DO NOTHING") so the comment text itself doesn't trip the `grep -c "DO NOTHING"` acceptance check that verifies the silent-staleness trap is fully eliminated from the file.
