<objective>
Enforce the out-of-sample holdout that the project documents but does not currently practice. Today the corpus orchestrator derives `TRAINING_WINDOW_END` as a bare `SELECT MAX(bar_ts) FROM feature_vectors` — every training, IC, and ensemble computation sees the full history including the supposedly-reserved OOS window. `alpha.validation.oos_start` (seeded `2025-12-24T05:15:00Z`) has ZERO readers anywhere in `src/`/`services/`/`scripts/`. This is the audit's single most important rigor gap: proving "ensemble IC > 0" (Phase 142A) on data that already saw the holdout would be a hollow gate.

Deliver three things: (1) the clamp in the corpus script so training never crosses the boundary; (2) a pre-committed governance doc fixing exactly how/when the holdout is scored and what "pass" means, written before anyone looks at OOS numbers; (3) a runnable, strictly read-only harness that scores the holdout window and reports qualifying-feature counts without mutating any corpus table.

This plan touches ONLY the corpus bash script and two new files. It deliberately does NOT modify `services/ic_engine.py` (reused by composition, not edited) so it cannot conflict with Plan 02 in the same wave.
</objective>
