<objective>
Eliminate the entire byte-for-byte duplicate of intelligence_features (feature_snapshots_shadow,
13 GB) and the parity_auditor service that compared the two tables. Replace the field-by-field
parity comparison with a lightweight SQL freshness function called from the existing
service_auditor_agent audit loop. This reclaims 13 GB immediately and removes 1.5 GB/week
of write amplification.

Purpose: Renaissance principle — every stored byte must earn its keep. The shadow table has never
detected a real violation. Insurance against a bug that does not exist.
Output: 13 GB reclaimed; 2 systemd services retired; 1 SQL function replaces 2 services.
</objective>
