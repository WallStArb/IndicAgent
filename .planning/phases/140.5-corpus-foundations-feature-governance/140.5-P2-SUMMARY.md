## Summary

Ran BIC K-selection study on 4 representative symbols (SPY, TLT, GLD, EWT) across K=2..6. K=5 won unanimously. Recorded decision in APR via migration 176, added `--refit` flag to regime_writer, and updated tests for the 5-label map.

## P2-T1: BIC K-Selection Study

BIC study script (`production/scripts/bic_k_selection.py`) fits GaussianHMM for each (symbol, K) pair on 5m 5D observation vectors (momentum, vol_of_vol, rel_volume, etc.) and selects the K that minimizes BIC.

### Results

| Symbol | Winner K | BIC |
|--------|----------|-----|
| SPY | 5 | -26,104,386 |
| TLT | 5 | -25,310,957 |
| GLD | 5 | -26,159,029 |
| EWT | 5 | -26,225,813 |

**Decision: K=5 unanimously.** K=6 overfit on all four symbols (BIC increased vs K=5).

APR key `feature.hmm.n_components` updated from 3 → 5 via migration 176.

