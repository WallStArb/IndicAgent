## Summary

Created `market_regimes` table, built `equity_regime_model.py` oneshot to compute cross-sectional equity regime labels (VIX proxy + breadth signals → 9 regimes), and wired ic_engine to join market_regimes and pool Spearman IC across all 58 ETFs per (tf, regime_label) cell.

## P4-T2: equity_regime_model.py

`services/equity_regime_model.py` oneshot:
- **VIX proxy**: SPY realized-vol z-score percentile rank (full-history rank, O(n log n), causal)
- **Breadth signal**: fraction of 58 equity ETFs with close > 200MA (pandas rolling)
- **9 regime labels**: `{low,mid,high}_{bull,neutral,bear}` from (vix_z_tertile × breadth_tertile)
- Populates all `(tf, ts)` combinations in feature_vectors corpus
- Upserts via partial-index ON CONFLICT clause

## P4-T3: ic_engine Cross-Sectional Pooling
