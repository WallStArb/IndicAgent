<objective>
Prove the fresh IC engine computes the same numbers as the stored pooled cells, then shrink
`feature_ic_scores` to the rows the phase keeps. A test-scoped parity harness replays each sampled
stored POOLED cell's observation set (label bars, peer symbols, (bar_ts, symbol) order, stride)
and computes point IC with the 186-10 `pooled_rank_ic` twice: with the table's targets, which must
reproduce the stored `ic_value` to float tolerance, and with kernel targets, where every difference
is attributed to gap rows or end-of-window NaNs with counts; 1h horizons 20/60 are excluded with a
count (D-21 as replaced by R-06). The report is committed. Then, in this order: the D-19 purge of
rows whose target window ends at or after `oos_start` (todo 439 IC part) and the delete of the
about 9.3M `is_pooled = false` old-grid rows (R-07), both under the performance SOP on the
compressed hypertable with a trailing VACUUM. Pooled rows stay until the fresh engine replaces
them (186-28, D-35). No gate beyond no ic_engine run.

Purpose: 186-23's one-change deletion of the old ic_engine is gated on this parity report, and
186-28 replaces the surviving pooled rows on rebuilt features. The purge is the last thing that
makes `feature_ic_scores` honest about the forward span before the old engine dies.

Output: parity harness with CI-clean synthetic tests, a committed parity report, the shrink
script, and the live post-state: 1,301,353 pooled rows, zero non-pooled rows, both deletes counted.
</objective>
