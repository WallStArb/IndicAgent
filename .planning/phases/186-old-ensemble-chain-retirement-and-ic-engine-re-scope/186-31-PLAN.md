<design>
**Steps.**
1. `data_gate`: the 584 rule, measured by a query this plan defines (no existing function covers
   it): for every active name whose 1d history starts before 2016, IBKR 5m rows in ohlcv_raw back
   to its 1d start, or an empty-history record for the missing span; Alpaca 5m from 2016 at its
   ceiling, defined as: per active name, the earliest ohlcv_raw row with vendor 'alpaca' and
   timeframe 5m is on or before greatest(2016-01-04, the name's first 1d session) plus five
   sessions. Records per-name counts and the failing list; refuses on any failure.
2. `pin_gate`: run inside the detached worktree at corpus_run.code_commit (created by --new-run,
   docs/reference/gotchas.md scratch-worktree procedure); HEAD equals code_commit and the rebuild
   code key equals corpus_run.code_key; no live-run process (D-32a); rebuild preconditions pass;
   the disk guard (R-09) for the next stage passes from live bar counts.
3. `pilot`: stage_1d's first symbol chunk at the pin (run_rebuild_stage chunk_indices=[0], 186-30),
   under the run id. Its units are ordinary
   full-universe units (same chunking), so they stay. G1-G5 run on them (G1-G3 the column screen
   on stored rows, G4-G5 ops_pilot_compute_gate). Records per-unit wall time, the extrapolated
   wall clock per stage, and DB load against the other lanes (D-32a).
4. `stage_1d` (resumes after the pilot chunk): the whole history in one pass; its rows spread
   over every chunk and stay uncompressed until each chunk's intraday rows are written too.
5. Per span s in order (Jan-1-aligned spans chosen at --new-run from live bar counts, see the
   budget): `s.1h`, `s.15m`, `s.5m`, each run_rebuild_stage(symbols=None, timeframes=[tf],
   run_id, span=s). After s.5m, compression_bound has moved past s's end, so s's chunks compress
   at that stage's end; the step fails unless every chunk whose range_end is at or before s's end
   is compressed (the chunk straddling the boundary compresses after the next span). The disk guard runs before each.
6. `feature_complete`: every planned unit of the full scope has a completed provenance row with
   this run_id; no started or failed row under the run; the column screen passes per tf.
7. `ic_measure` under the run id, then `d19_bound` (no feature_ic_scores row at or after
   alpha.validation.oos_start), then `canary --run-id` (186-30).

**Recovery.** A unit completed under another run id (or a stale started row) fails
feature_complete with its batch keys listed. The runbook's procedure: supersede those provenance
rows through the sanctioned completed -> superseded transition (migration 416), delete their rows
with the unit's replace_where, and retry the stage step under the same run id; the runner never
does this silently.

**Interface.** --new-run (creates corpus_run and the worktree, prints the run id); --run-id X
--step S; --run-id X --through S. A completed step re-invoked is a no-op reporting its stored
outcome; a failed step retries after its cause is fixed and never skips ahead. Logs to
logs/corpus_rerun.log; job_completed_total{job, status} per step.
</design>

<disk_budget>
Measured: 1,379 bytes per stored row uncompressed, indexes included (186-26 1d pilot, 324 columns;
311 now); compressed at the old table's ~6x, about 230 bytes per row. Free 699 GB (2026-11-05);
reserve plus margin 50 GB. Chunks are time-only and shared by every tf, and compression_bound holds
any chunk a pending unit of the full scope can still write, so nothing compresses until every tf of
a time span is written. The budget is therefore per span, not per tf.

Projection at the 534 target (2006-2026 5m for every name; 186-26 counts scaled by about 1.9 for
the pre-2016 intraday history; 1d unchanged):

| tf | Rows | Uncompressed | Compressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1d | 7.1M | 10 GB | 2 GB |
| 1h | about 57M | about 79 GB | about 13 GB |
| 15m | about 208M | about 287 GB | about 48 GB |
| 5m | about 600M | about 830 GB | about 138 GB |
| all | about 870M | about 1,200 GB | about 200 GB |

Two spans (about 600 GB uncompressed each) peak near 600 + 100 compressed + 50 reserve = 750 GB:
over 699. Three Jan-1-aligned spans of about equal rows (about 400 GB each) peak during the last
span near 400 + 135 compressed + the 1d rows + spool + 50 reserve, about 600 GB: fits. --new-run
picks the span count from live bar counts as the smallest n where the largest span plus the
compressed rest plus the reserve fits free disk, and the pin_gate disk guard re-checks before
every stage and refuses short.
</disk_budget>

<failure_modes>
What fails silently, and what stops it:
- A gate passes on rows from another run: every gate reads by run_id (186-30), so a cell or unit
  without it fails the gate.
- A unit completed under an earlier run is skipped and counted as done: feature_complete counts
  only this run's completed units.
- A subset stage never compresses or keys units differently: no --symbols, ever; the pilot is the
  real first chunk.
- Code moves between stages: the detached worktree pins the code; pin_gate re-checks before every
  stage, and the fingerprinted writer refuses a changed key.
- Bars move under the run: the run's horizon pins every unit's reads; a revised bar renames its
  unit, which fails completeness loudly.
- Disk runs out mid-stage: per-stage disk guard; spans keep each stage under the budget.
- A step is skipped on resume (the old --from-step trap): no skip flag exists.
</failure_modes>
