## Task 1: Per-TF ATR Multiplier Caps

**File:** `src/intelligence/trading/trade_framer.py`

Added `ATR_TARGET_MAX_MULTIPLIER_BY_TF` dict mapping timeframes to max ATR multiplier caps:
- 1m: 3x (was 8x)
- 5m: 5x (was 8x)
- 15m: 7x (was 8x)
- 1h/4h/1d: 8x (unchanged)

Updated `_collect_targets_long()` and `_collect_targets_short()` to look up per-TF cap from features dict, falling back to 8x for unknown timeframes.

**Rationale:** Lower timeframes on volatile futures (ES, NQ) frequently produce structural targets at 6-8x ATR that are unreachable within the bar's lifetime. Tighter caps on 1m/5m/15m prevent signals with unrealistic profit targets from being published.
