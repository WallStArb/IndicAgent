<objective>
Close the ETF exposure gaps D-06 actually names, correctly: add the missing factor and vol
exposure vocabulary and retag the momentum/quality/low-vol ETFs the corpus already owns, and
record the evidence-backed ticker decision for the two exposures that genuinely have no
instrument at all (EM currency, volatility). Plan 10 performs the onboarding and backfill of
those two tickers.

Purpose: D-06 asks for momentum and quality factor-tilt representation on the premise that there
is currently zero. That premise is false — RESEARCH.md falsified it with a live query, and it
re-verifies here: MTUM, QUAL and USMV were added 2026-05-15, are `is_active=true`, and carry
2.1-2.4M OHLCV rows each. What is actually missing is identifiability: all three sit under a
single coarse `eq_factor` tag shared with BTAL (market-neutral) and SPHB (high-beta) — five
structurally different strategies under one label, which makes factor-specific IC stratification
impossible. So D-06's intent is delivered by a tag-taxonomy change, not a sourcing/backfill
change, and none of Plan 05/09's newly-freed compute headroom is spent on redundant instruments.
The two genuinely empty exposures (zero `fx_em`-tagged symbols; zero VIX-linked instrument of
any kind) are the only real sourcing work.

Output: migration 338 (applied live + committed) and a decision document naming the two tickers
with the evidence behind each.
</objective>
