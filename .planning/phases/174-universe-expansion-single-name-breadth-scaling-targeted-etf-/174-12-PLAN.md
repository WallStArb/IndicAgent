<objective>
Draw the market-cap-stratified Russell 3000 sample at the size Plan 11 measured, onboard it,
launch its backfill, and promote symbols to compute-eligible as their history lands — the phase's
actual breadth-scaling deliverable (D-01/D-02/D-03).

Purpose: the personal-scale edge determination program closed with the verdict that this corpus,
at effective breadth ~8.4, cannot carry the endgame, and prescribed single-name breadth expansion
as the primary lever — IR ≈ IC × √breadth, with IC small and stable (~0.03-0.06) and breadth the
dominant term. Every preceding plan in this phase exists to make this run safe: the memory fix so
ic_engine survives the scale, the governance split so hundreds of new names do not silently become
live-tradeable, the onboarding helper so none of them lands half-written, and the stratified
sampler so the down-cap hypothesis is tested rather than assumed.

Output: the drawn sample onboarded and backfilling, a promotion tool, and a reproducibility record
tying the sample to the exact file, seed and parameters that produced it.

## What "complete" means for Phase 174 — three tiers, and this plan claims only the first

IBKR paces historical requests at roughly 60 per 10 minutes. Backfilling several hundred new
symbols across `5m`/`15m`/`1h`/`1d` therefore takes **days**, not hours. When this plan finishes,
the promotion tool will run and promote almost nothing, because almost nothing will have finished
all four timeframes yet. That is the expected and correct outcome, not a failure — but it must be
unmissable rather than inferred:

| Tier | Name | What is true | Who closes it |
|------|------|--------------|---------------|
| **(a)** | **Phase 174 implementation complete** | Sample drawn and recorded; every qualifying symbol onboarded across all four tables at `compute_eligible=false, live_tradeable=false`; backfill launched under supervision; promotion tool exists and has been run once | **This plan. This is the only tier Phase 174 claims.** |
| (b) | Phase 174 data complete | Backfill finished for the sampled universe; the promotion tool has promoted them to `compute_eligible=true` | Post-phase, tracked through `backfill_status`; re-run the promotion tool as symbols land |
| (c) | Phase 174 empirical complete | `ic_engine` recompute finished over the expanded corpus and effective breadth re-measured | Post-phase, a separate tracked run |

Until tier (c) lands, **~8.4 remains the last measured effective breadth**. A larger `instruments`
count is not a larger effective breadth, and this plan must not state or imply otherwise. The
precedent is exact: Phase 173 completed 2026-08-26 and its corpus recompute completed 2026-08-31,
tracked separately.
