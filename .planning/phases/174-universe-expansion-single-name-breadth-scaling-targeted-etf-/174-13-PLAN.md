<objective>
Add a purely additive fourth eligibility dimension — `compute_eligible_1d` on `instruments`, a
1d-only compute-readiness predicate beside Plan 02's four-timeframe one, and
`get_active_contracts(dimension='compute_1d')` — so the D-09 down-cap cohort can be backfilled
and measured at 1d without ever becoming visible to the 5m/15m/1h cross-sectional cells.

Purpose: D-09 restricts the down-cap stratified sample to 1d bars. Those symbols will have no
5m/15m/1h history at all. The guarantee this plan must deliver is structural, not conventional: a
1d-only symbol must be *unable* to appear in a `dimension='compute'` result, because it does not
carry `compute_eligible`, rather than being filtered out by every consumer correctly remembering
to check timeframe presence. A convention that every call site must honor is exactly the failure
class D-07 exists to end (`is_active` conflating three meanings across 37 call sites).

Output: one additive migration, one additive predicate constant, one additive dimension value, and
a regression test proving the three pre-existing dimensions did not move.
</objective>
