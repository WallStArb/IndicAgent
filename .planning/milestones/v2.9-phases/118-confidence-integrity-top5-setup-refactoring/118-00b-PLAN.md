Purpose: These three plugins do not have a simple extrinsic call site to delete — their confidence is a weighted composite that mixed extrinsic terms (regime_score, trend_regime, ctf_score) and zone/exhaustion friction into the formula. Removing those requires reweighting the surviving intrinsic factors, which is substantively different work from the mechanical deletions in 118-00. This plan depends on 118-00 — the deletion sweep across the other 12 plugins must be committed first so the contract test runs against the fully-stripped codebase.

Output: 3 restructured plugin files with intrinsic composites summing to 1.0, factor-clamped, routed through compose_confidence; one new parametrized contract test file; all unit tests green.
</objective>
