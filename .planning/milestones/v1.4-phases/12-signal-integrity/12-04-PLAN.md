<objective>
Extend signal_ledger query and signal_lifecycle_service to track shadow signals (SIGINT-05 completion).

Purpose: Regime-suppressed signals already appear in signal_ledger (Plan 03). Now the lifecycle service must pick them up at startup and track their counterfactual MAE/MFE/outcome. Shadow signals are "virtually activated" from bar close — their lifecycle runs exactly like an active signal, but status never changes from regime_suppressed, and the 8-class outcome tells us "what would have happened" if the gate hadn't suppressed them. This feedback data is what makes the gate threshold empirically tunable.
