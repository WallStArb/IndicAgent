1. ATR bug fixed + full mathematical audit — all 28 I1 indicators validated, edge cases, CI gates

6. Infrastructure hygiene (Renaissance) — 9/9 HYGIENE criteria: BaseAgent adoption, metric types, writer flush spans, shadow integrity, DAG completeness
