<objective>
Remove the `is_backfill` guard from `_ingest_signal` in `SignalTrackerComputeAgent`.

Purpose: The guard `if canonical["is_backfill"] and bars_elapsed >= canonical["ttl_bars"]` prevents real-time signals loaded at startup (where `is_backfill=False`) from taking the fast-path TTL expiry. After a restart, these signals sit in the active index waiting for Kafka bars that will never arrive (minimal retention). This is the root cause of the pending signal backlog.

Output: A one-line conditional change that makes the TTL fast-path apply to ALL signals at ingest time, not only those flagged as backfill.
</objective>
