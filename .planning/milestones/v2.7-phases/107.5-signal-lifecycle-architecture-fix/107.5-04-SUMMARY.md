## Decisions Made

- **No ELSE default in CASE expression:** Unknown timeframes are excluded from the `WHERE` clause rather than coerced to 60s. The reconciliation step fails with `sys.exit(1)` if any unknown-TF rows remain. This enforces D-03 fail-loud design.
- **View recreation required:** `signal_ledger_full` enumerates columns explicitly (no `SELECT *`). A bare `ADD COLUMN` on the base table does not propagate to the view. The migration drops and recreates the view to expose `sl.expires_at` for Plan 107.5-06's replay auditor.
- **Backfill is self-contained:** No JOIN to `intelligence_features` or other tables is needed. `timestamp`, `ttl_bars`, and `feature_tf` are all present in `signal_ledger`, keeping the backfill simple and fast.
