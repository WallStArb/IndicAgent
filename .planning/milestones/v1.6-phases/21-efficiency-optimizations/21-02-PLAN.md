self.bar_history: dict[str, deque] = defaultdict(lambda: deque(maxlen=200))

# Deque behavior

When deque.append() is called on a full deque (len == maxlen):
- Oldest item is automatically removed
- len() remains constant (maxlen)
- NO event/notification is emitted

This means we must track overflow explicitly by comparing length before/after.

# Optimization target

Only invalidate _df_cache when deque overflows (oldest bar removed):
- When len(deque) < maxlen after append: cache remains valid
- When len(deque) == maxlen after append: cache is invalidated (overflow occurred)
