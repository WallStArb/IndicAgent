**One-liner:** Standalone `BarAggregatorComputeAgent(BaseAgent)` consuming `market.bars`, accumulating via `BarAccumulator`, publishing completed HTF bars to `market.bars.htf` on port :9120 with Golden Signals metrics and systemd unit enabled.

## What Was Built

**`services/bar_aggregator_agent.py`** — `BarAggregatorComputeAgent(BaseAgent)`:
- Subscribes to `topic_market_bars(env)` (1m bars from DataProviderAgent)
- Publishes to `topic_market_bars_htf(env)` (HTF bars for FeatureComputeAgent)
- Consumer group: `bar_aggregator_consumer`, `auto_offset_reset="latest"` (D-14)
- Delegates accumulation to `BarAccumulator` — no copy of logic, clean SoC
- Dual-format bar parsing: `BarMessage.model_validate(payload)` first, legacy flat-dict fallback
- Golden Signals on port :9120: `bar_agg_events_consumed_total`, `bar_agg_htf_bars_produced_total{tf}`, `bar_agg_aggregation_latency_seconds`, `bar_agg_aggregation_errors_total`
- Exception isolation: errors logged and counted; single bad bar never crashes the agent
- `init_tracing("bar_aggregator_agent")` in `__main__` — OTel spans ready for Grafana Tempo

**`services/indicagent-bar-aggregator-compute.service`** — systemd unit:
- `After=indicagent-data-provider.service` — enforces D-18 startup ordering
- `PYTHONUNBUFFERED=1` and `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT` — consistent with fleet
- `systemctl enable` executed — service enabled, symlink confirmed in multi-user.target
