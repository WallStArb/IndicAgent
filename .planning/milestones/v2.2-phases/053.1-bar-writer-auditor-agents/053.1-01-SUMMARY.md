**Task 2 — BarWriterAgent:**
- `BarWriterAgent(BaseAgent)` — WriterAgent class with metrics port :9121
- Subscribes to `market.bars` + `market.bars.htf` via single `KafkaConsumerClient(*topics)` multi-topic subscription
- Buffers incoming bars as 9-tuples `(ts, symbol, tf, open, high, low, close, volume, source)`
- Tags source: `"live_1m"` for 1m bars, `"live_htf"` for all HTF bars (D-04)

- 6 Golden Signal metrics: `events_consumed_total`, `bars_written_total{tf}`, `persistence_batch_latency_seconds`, `write_errors_total`, `conflict_skips_total`, `persistence_consumer_lag`
- SIGTERM drain: `_teardown()` flushes remaining buffer before closing pool + consumer
