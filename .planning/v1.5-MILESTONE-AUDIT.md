### Integration Gaps (2)

**Gap 1 — Medium:** `ai_narrative_service._per_signal_timeout` bypasses `Settings.llm_timeout_sec`
- Affected: API-02, API-04
- Location: `services/ai_narrative_service.py:574`
- Root cause: `_build_chains()` reads `pcfg["openrouter_timeout_sec"]` (default 30.0s) instead of `Settings().llm_timeout_sec` (default 60.0s, env-configurable). The explicit `timeout=` arg passed to `generate()` overrides `self.timeout` stored by providers.
- Fix: `self._per_signal_timeout = Settings().llm_timeout_sec` at line 574
- Impact: `LLM_TIMEOUT_SEC` env var has no runtime effect on narrative calls. Non-blocking for current deployment (30.0s is a reasonable default).

**Gap 2 — Info:** `CIRCUIT_BREAKER_OPEN_SECONDS` histogram never invoked
- Affected: CB-04 (partial tracking)
- Location: `src/observability/metrics.py:141`
- Documented as intentional in Phase 20 SUMMARY. Empty histogram at Prometheus endpoint. No alert breakage.
